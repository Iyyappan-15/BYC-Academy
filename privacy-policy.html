🔒 Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2024

← Go Back 📅 Effective: April 2024

At BYC Academy, we care about your privacy. This page explains in simple words what information we collect, why we collect it, and how we keep it safe. If you have any questions, feel free to contact us anytime.

What Information We Collect

When you use our website or enroll with us, we may collect:

  • Your name and contact number (when you fill out our contact or enrollment form)
  • Your email address (if you provide it)
  • Your child's grade/class (to suggest the right program)
  • Basic usage data like which pages you visit (collected automatically, without identifying you personally)

Why We Collect It

We only collect information to:

  • Respond to your inquiries or enrollment requests
  • Share updates about classes, schedules, or new programs
  • Improve our website and services

We do not sell your information to anyone. Ever.

How We Keep It Safe

We store your information securely and only allow authorised staff to access it. We use standard security measures to protect against unauthorised access.

Do We Share Your Information?

No. We do not share your personal information with third parties, except where required by law (for example, if a government authority requests it).

How Long We Keep It

We keep your information only as long as needed — usually for the duration of your enrollment and a short period after, for record-keeping purposes.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Ask us what information we hold about you
  • Request that we correct or delete your information
  • Opt out of receiving messages from us

Just reach out to us at our contact page and we'll take care of it promptly.

Children's Privacy

Our website is designed for general audiences including parents and students. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13 without parental consent.

Questions?

If you have any concerns about your privacy, please contact us through our Contact Us page. We're happy to help.
