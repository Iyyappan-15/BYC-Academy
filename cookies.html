This page explains what cookies are, how BYC Academy uses them on our website, and what choices you have. Don't worry — we keep things simple and we don't use cookies to track you in any harmful way.

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are tiny files that a website saves on your browser (like Chrome or Safari) when you visit. They help the website remember things — like your preferences — so your experience is smoother the next time you visit.

Think of them like a sticky note that the website keeps to remember you. They are not viruses or programs — they can't harm your device.

What Cookies Do We Use?

⚙️

Essential Cookies

These make the website work properly. Without them, some parts of the site won't function. We always use these.

📊

Analytics Cookies

These help us understand how visitors use our site — like which pages are popular. This helps us improve the website.

🗺️

Third-Party Cookies

Services like Google Maps (used on our Contact page) may set their own cookies. We don't control those.

We Do NOT Use

  • Advertising or marketing cookies
  • Cookies that track you across other websites
  • Cookies that collect sensitive personal information

How to Manage Cookies

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Here's how to do it in popular browsers:

  • Google Chrome: Settings → Privacy and Security → Cookies
  • Safari: Settings → Safari → Privacy
  • Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies
  • Edge: Settings → Privacy → Cookies

If you block all cookies, some parts of our website may not work as expected — but the basic information will still be available to you.

Changes to This Policy

If we ever change how we use cookies, we'll update this page and the date at the top. We recommend checking here occasionally if you're curious.
